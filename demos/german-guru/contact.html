Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about joining ANVI GERMAN MASTER.

1. How do I book a Free Demo Class with Trainer Anvi?
You can fill out the Demo Class form on our website or simply WhatsApp us at +91 8283945753. Our team will schedule your free live trial class at your convenient time.
2. Are classes available online as well as offline?
Yes! We conduct both offline small-batch classes at our campus and HD live interactive Zoom studio webinars for students located across India, Germany, and worldwide.
3. What is the average batch size at ANVI GERMAN MASTER?
We maintain strictly limited batch sizes of maximum 8 to 10 students to ensure every learner gets individual attention, daily speaking practice, and direct feedback from Trainer Anvi.
4. Do you assist with Goethe-Zertifikat and TELC exam registration?
Absolutely! We provide complete assistance with official Goethe-Zertifikat, TELC, ÖSD, and DELF exam slot bookings, exam pattern orientation, and past paper mock tests.
5. What is the eligibility for the Germany Ausbildung program?
Candidates who have completed 12th grade or a diploma/degree, aged between 18 to 30, and possess German B1 or B2 language certification are eligible for paid Ausbildung programs in Nursing, IT, Hospitality, and Mechatronics.
