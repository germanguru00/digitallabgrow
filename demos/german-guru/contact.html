Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining GERMAN GURU classes in Chandigarh or online.

1. How do I book a Free Demo Class at GERMAN GURU?

You can simply click the "Book Free Demo" button on any page or WhatsApp us directly at +91 6280723651. We will arrange a free 1-on-1 trial slot so you can experience our interactive teaching methodology firsthand.

2. Where is the GERMAN GURU campus located?

Our flagship offline campus is centrally situated at SCO Number 80-81-82, 1st Floor, Corner Building, Sector 34A, Chandigarh - 160034. Sector 34A is the primary education landmark of the tri-city with excellent parking and transport connectivity.

3. Are classes available online as well as offline?

Yes, absolutely! We provide both offline small-batch classes at our Sector 34A campus and high-definition live interactive online masterclasses via Zoom for students across India, Germany, UAE, and worldwide. All online classes are 100% two-way interactive with full access to cloud session recordings.

4. What is the batch size at GERMAN GURU?

We maintain a strictly enforced limit of maximum 8 to 10 students per batch. This guarantees that every single student has daily speaking opportunities in German, personalized attention for case endings, and line-by-line correction of written assignments.

5. Do you assist with official Goethe-Zertifikat and TELC exam registration?

Yes! We provide complete assistance with Goethe-Institut (Max Mueller Bhavan) and TELC exam slot booking. Because exam seats in Delhi and Chandigarh fill within minutes, our desk alerts you and helps you secure your exam date without hassle.

6. What are the requirements for the Germany Ausbildung program?

Candidates who have passed 12th standard (or hold a diploma/degree), generally aged between 18 to 30 years, and hold a certified German B1 or B2 level are eligible for paid Ausbildung programs in Nursing, IT, Mechatronics, and Hospitality. There are zero tuition fees and you earn 800€ to 1,300€ every month from Day 1.
