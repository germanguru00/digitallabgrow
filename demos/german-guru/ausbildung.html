Dual Vocational Education

What is Germany Ausbildung?

Ausbildung is Germany's world-renowned dual vocational training program that combines classroom theory at a vocational college (Berufsschule) with practical hands-on work at a German company.

Unlike traditional university degrees, Ausbildung trainees do not pay tuition fees—instead, trainees receive a regular monthly salary (Stipend) from the employer starting from day one!
